# Issue #20: star powers, step 1 (probe)

Measured on main fe49fe2 + this branch, 2026-11-24, with `python -m star_audit` (host built with `star.c`, system qemu).
Two runs: all 212 battle-pool cards x 3 native-AI battles of 3000 ticks (64 battles), then the 27 cards that were
"asked" or had a battle-logic call site x 10 battles of 4000 ticks (27 battles). No card was rejected and the
"cards off" byte (player+0x238) was 0 for every player.

## Result

| Class | Cards | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| read | 15 | the battle logic looked the card up on its carrier: a live reader (these are `star_audit.LIVE`) |
| asked | 3 | its type was looked up, never on the carrier's config |
| generic | 11 | no lookup, but the row has Traits / Components (attached at spawn; not measured) |
| none, sim site | 22 | a reader exists in the battle logic (often a virtual method), but it never ran in 91 sim-only battles |
| none, ui only | 16 | the only readers are client code (menus, aim indicators): never runs in training |
| none, no site | 145 | no call with that type anywhere in libg: compiled out |
| skipped | 3 | Lightyear's alt-character cards: a form, not in the battle pool |

So 15 of 215 star powers work in training today (as far as a lookup shows; each card's effect is not yet A/B tested),
and at least 145 + 16 = 161 have no reader that can run in a battle.

## What it would take
- **The 22 with a sim site:** find what calls the reader in the real game. Most sit in one family of small functions at
  0xb3020c-0xb30620 (medikit, faster_long_dash, explosion_damage_increase, attack_pierce, ...) with no direct callers:
  vtable slots. Hook one (medikit 0xb3020c) in a battle with the client on and off to see whether only the client calls
  it; if so, calling it for every sim character at spawn may turn several on at once. Size S to M.
- **The 161 without a reader:** re-implement in C by group as issue #20 step 3 says (damage modifiers and heals first,
  through `env.c my_root` and the heal path), reading Value1..6 with 0xc34c6c. Size L.
- **The 3 asked and the 11 generic:** a per-card A/B check (with and without the card, the expected change from its
  Value columns) decides them. That check is also what should confirm the 15 before training leans on them.
- Gears: unchanged, still no host field.

## Per-card hits (follow-up to #34, closed #33)

`star.c` counted lookups per card Type enum, so a hit on a type counted for every carried card of that type. Two star
powers can share a Type (Gale's Blower_unique and Damian's Gladiator_unique are both stun_wall_push, enum 87), and the
config's +0x50 list also holds gadget and gear cards. `star.c` now also logs, for every hit, the card that answered and the
player slot whose config it was (`[star] h` lines). `star_audit` counts a card as read only when it answered itself, and
reports the hits another card of its type answered as `shared_hits`.

Measured on main 7c84d84 + this branch, 2026-11-24:
- All 215 cards, 3 battles of 3000 ticks each (64 battles): read 13, asked 4, generic 11, none 184, skipped 3. The two
  LIVE cards not read here (BowDude_unique_2 stun_trap, FutureGirl_unique_2 pet_extra_health) wait for rare events:
  the longer run reads both.
- The 15 LIVE cards plus Gladiator_unique, Baseball_unique_2 and Cocooner_unique, 10 battles of 4000 ticks each: all 15
  LIVE cards read, every hit on the carrier's own config (JetpackGirl_unique also has 10 hits while the battle was being
  built, before the slots are known). Gladiator_unique: asked, its 9 type hits were all answered by Gale's card.

So `LIVE` stays as it is. Jessie, Wendy and Gale (and Damian) were "read for others" in #33 because #33 matched the
player-level getters per type and did not hook 0xc34d10; here each hit is the carrier's own card. Damian's card is the
one the type count would have turned into a false "read" whenever it shared a battle with Gale's.
